# Review Entry Template

Status: current optional history template. Create history lazily only after an actual review and only when useful.

```markdown
## Review — YYYY-MM-DD

Knowledge ID: `<id>`
Type: <FULL | TARGETED | QUESTIONS_ONLY | MAP_REFRESH | SOURCE_REPAIR | FORMATIVE | other useful label>
Scope: <whole Review Scope or exact subset>
Retention band at review: <current advisory band>

### Before opening the Unit

- Recalled:
- Uncertain / missed:

Optional recall score: <0–4 or —>

### Checked findings

- <MEMORY_GAP | KNOWLEDGE_BASE_GAP | NEW_QUESTION> — <finding>

### Next action

Next review: <YYYY-MM-DD or —>
Next type/scope: <text or —>
Reason/context: <optional>
```

The template is intentionally light. Do not require every field when it adds no value, and do not create a formal state-transition protocol around review history.
