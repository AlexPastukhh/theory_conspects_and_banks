# UC-KNOWLEDGE-PLAN-EXPANSION — Plan Knowledge Expansion

## Situation
There is a need to decide what to learn next.

The trigger may be a coverage gap, new Area, Question, repetition finding, practice, source material, analogy, captured learning batch, or ordinary curiosity.

## Result
A structured, contextualized, and prioritized set of expansion work.

Meaningful targets:
- are attached to an appropriate Domain / Area / nested-Area context;
- may be an Area, Concept, responsibility gap, Question, or bounded cluster of related Questions;
- are decomposed when useful;
- have sufficiently clear scope;
- receive priority;
- receive a useful disposition such as `UNSCHEDULED`, `PURSUE_NOW`, `DEFER`, `DISCARD`, or `NEEDS_CLARIFICATION`;
- may be inserted into the Expansion Map when explicitly selected for action, optionally with lightweight temporal/order placement (`today / tomorrow / later`, a date, or another simple ordering).

Planning is independently useful and does not require immediate research. Temporal placement is optional: semantic placement and priority remain valid even when no date is assigned.

## Process
1. Capture the original learning need.
2. Locate it in the knowledge map.
3. Identify the underlying responsibility/Key Question before choosing a technology/tool-specific target.
4. Attach concrete Questions to an Area and related Concept(s) when independent planning/lifecycle identity is useful. A coverage gap itself may remain the target without a Question entity.
5. Decompose broad Areas/responsibilities into useful Questions/coverage gaps where useful.
6. Check existing knowledge, responsibility coverage, relevant manifestations, and duplicate Questions.
7. Evaluate targets using the Priority Model.
8. Determine useful order and required depth.
9. For explicitly selected actionable targets, add them to the Expansion Map; time/order context is optional.
10. Preserve deferred targets with context.
11. Route selected targets to the appropriate learning Use Case.

## Boundary
This Use Case answers:

> What should be learned, in what scope, with what priority, and—when useful—in what planned order/time slot?

It does not have to answer the Questions itself.

It also does not coordinate Expansion Map work against Repetition or Capture/Triage; those remain separate operational concerns.
