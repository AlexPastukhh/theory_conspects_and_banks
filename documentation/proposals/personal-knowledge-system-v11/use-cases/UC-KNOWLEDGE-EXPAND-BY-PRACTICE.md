# UC-KNOWLEDGE-EXPAND-BY-PRACTICE — Expand Through Practice

## Situation
Important knowledge should be tested, developed, or challenged through application.

## Result
Evidence about the ability to apply the model and about real behavior:
- predictions are tested;
- failures/surprises surface;
- incorrect assumptions become visible;
- practical Questions appear;
- durable reusable findings are extracted.

A single practice session does not automatically prove mastery.

## Process
1. Define the knowledge the practice should exercise.
2. Use Priority to choose appropriate depth.
3. Design a task where the relevant mechanism actually matters.
4. State predictions/decisions before execution when useful.
5. Perform the work.
6. Delegate mechanical parts to AI only when this does not destroy the learning objective.
7. Observe behavior, failures, surprises, and difficult decisions.
8. Separate durable knowledge from project-specific noise.
9. Integrate durable findings through the shared process.
10. Route new Questions to expansion planning.
11. Let Learning State and Retention/Repetition policy decide when the resulting knowledge enters normal repetition; do not schedule raw practice observations directly.

## Boundary
Simply completing a project is not sufficient. Practice must have a learning objective or produce meaningful feedback.
