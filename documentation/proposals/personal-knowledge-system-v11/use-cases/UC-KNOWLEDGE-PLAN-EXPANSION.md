# UC-KNOWLEDGE-PLAN-EXPANSION — Plan Knowledge Expansion

## Situation
There is a need to decide what to learn next.

The trigger may be a coverage gap, new Area, Question, repetition finding, practice, source material, analogy, captured learning batch, or ordinary curiosity.

## Result
A structured, contextualized, and prioritized set of expansion work.

Meaningful targets:
- are attached to an appropriate Domain / nested Area context;
- may be an Area, Concept, Question, or bounded cluster of related Questions;
- are decomposed when useful;
- have sufficiently clear scope;
- receive priority;
- receive a work disposition such as `PURSUE_NOW`, `DEFER`, `DISCARD`, or `NEEDS_CLARIFICATION`;
- may receive lightweight temporal/order placement in the Expansion Plan when selected for action (`today / tomorrow / later`, a date, or another simple ordering).

Planning is independently useful and does not require immediate research. Temporal placement is optional: semantic placement and priority remain valid even when no date is assigned.

## Process
1. Capture the original learning need.
2. Locate it in the knowledge map.
3. Attach concrete Questions to an Area and related Concept(s) when known.
4. Decompose broad Areas into useful Questions/coverage gaps where useful.
5. Check existing knowledge and duplicate Questions.
6. Evaluate targets using the Priority Model.
7. Determine useful order and required depth.
8. For selected actionable targets, place them in the Expansion Plan when time/order context is useful.
9. Preserve deferred targets with context.
10. Route selected targets to the appropriate learning Use Case.

## Boundary
This Use Case answers:

> What should be learned, in what scope, with what priority, and—when useful—in what planned order/time slot?

It does not have to answer the Questions itself.

It also does not coordinate Expansion Plan work against Repetition or Capture/Triage; those remain separate operational concerns.
